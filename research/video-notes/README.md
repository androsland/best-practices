## Method

- Moviola produced timestamped local Whisper transcripts for 26 videos.
- The silent AIVM Brain reel was analyzed from 12 scene-aware frames.
- Three clips received additional frame inspection to resolve names or visually demonstrated behavior.
- Related ideas were merged rather than repeated clip by clip.
- Security recommendations were checked against primary documentation where the reels were incomplete or overly absolute.

## Evidence convention

References such as `S01 00:46–01:43` point to the [source index](source-index.md). A reference means the video made or demonstrated that point; it is not automatically independent proof of a marketing claim.

The social-media clips contain promotional language, rounded statistics, and occasionally imprecise technical prescriptions. The synthesis separates reusable guidance from claims that still require product, repository, licensing, security, or pricing verification.
